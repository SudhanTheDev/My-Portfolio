Tarmiles Action is a high-contrast italic display face with dry-brush edges and a hard forward charge to every letter.

The verticals are thick and chiselled; the horizontals taper into thin tails. The whole alphabet pulls to the right, and the rough perimeter — the small splatters and broken edges — comes from a loaded brush dragged at speed across uncoated paper. There is a sharp 60s comic energy in the caps and a mid-century sports-magazine bite in the way the letters cut across the line.

It hammers nicely across the cover of a paperback motor-racing pulp, or barked over a freeze-frame in the opening title of a low-budget action film.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
